# Service levels

The ERP database (Google Cloud SQL) must be restored within 15 minutes of an outage.
The ERP database must be available for 99.5 percent of each month.
The MQTT broker must be restored within 5 minutes of an outage.
The MQTT broker must be available for 99.9 percent of each month.
The reflow oven must be brought back below its temperature limit within 10 minutes.
