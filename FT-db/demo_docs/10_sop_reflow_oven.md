# SOP-OVN-001 Reflow oven OVN1

Version 2, approved by the line supervisor.

1. The reflow oven OVN1 normally runs at 235 degrees Celsius.
2. The oven temperature must not exceed 260 degrees Celsius. If it does, lower the setpoint to 235 and call the supervisor.
3. Do not start production when the oven temperature is below 230 degrees Celsius.
4. The oven temperature is published to the MQTT broker every 2 seconds.
