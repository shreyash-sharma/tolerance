# SOP-TR-001 Operating the transfer robot (TR)

Version 4, approved by the line supervisor.

1. Before starting a shift, check that the guard on the TR cell is closed.
2. The arm must not run above 80 percent of its maximum speed during normal production.
3. Preventive maintenance on TR is only allowed between 02:00 and 04:00 local time.
4. After 3 consecutive faults, TR must be stopped and maintenance called. Do not restart it from the panel.
5. The gripper must be inspected every 500 hours.
